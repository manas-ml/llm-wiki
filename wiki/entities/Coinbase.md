# Coinbase

**Type**: organization
**Also known as**: Coinbase Global, Inc.

## Overview

Coinbase is the leading U.S. cryptocurrency exchange, managing $226 billion in quarterly trading volume. They are cited in [[Building Effective AI Agents]] as a flagship enterprise AI agent deployment — their Claude-powered agentic customer support system handles thousands of messages per hour at 99.99% availability.
