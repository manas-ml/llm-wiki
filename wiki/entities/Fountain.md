# Fountain

**Type**: organization
**Also known as**: Fountain Inc.

## Overview

Fountain is a frontline workforce management platform cited in the *2026 Agentic Coding Trends Report* as a case study for hierarchical multi-agent orchestration in HR and hiring workflows. Their "Fountain Copilot" uses a central orchestration agent to coordinate specialist agents across candidate screening, onboarding, and conversion workflows.
