# Vannevar Bush

**Type**: person
**Also known as**: Van Bush

## Overview

Vannevar Bush (1890–1974) was an American engineer, inventor, and science administrator. During World War II he led the U.S. Office of Scientific Research and Development. He is best known in computing history for his 1945 essay "As We May Think," which described the [[Memex]] — a hypothetical personal knowledge device with associative trails between documents, widely considered the conceptual precursor to hypertext and the World Wide Web.

## Role in This Wiki

Bush's Memex is the historical precursor to the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]]. His vision of a private, actively curated knowledge store with associative trails maps directly onto this wiki's architecture. The LLM solves the maintenance problem Bush left open.
