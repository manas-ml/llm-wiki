# Anthropic

**Type**: organization
**Also known as**: Anthropic PBC

## Overview

Anthropic is an AI safety company and the creator of Claude. Founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers including Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei. Anthropic builds large language models with a focus on safety and interpretability. Claude is their primary model family, powering the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] system this wiki runs on.

## Role in This Wiki

Anthropic is both the creator of the model (Claude) that maintains this wiki and the author of [[Building Effective AI Agents]], a primary source on AI agent architecture patterns.
