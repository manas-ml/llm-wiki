# Rakuten

**Type**: organization
**Also known as**: Rakuten Group, Inc.

## Overview

Rakuten is a Japanese multinational e-commerce and technology company, one of the largest in the world. Cited in *Scaling Agentic Coding Across Your Organization* alongside [[Brex]] as an organization building specialized, domain-specific agents on top of the Claude Code SDK — extending agentic coding capabilities into transformative business processes unique to their domain.
